Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cukka Creatives (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use MorphMe (“the app”). MorphMe does not require account registration.

1. Information we process

  • Photos and generated images: Portraits you take or select, and the AI-generated transformation results created from them.
  • App preferences and identifiers: Language, selected or detected country/region, premium status, usage state, app version, and an app-specific device identifier.
  • Advertising data: Advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location derived from network signals, and ad interaction data may be processed by Google AdMob.
  • Purchase data: Product identifiers, purchase state, subscription status, and purchase tokens supplied by Google Play. We do not receive your complete payment-card details.
  • Analytics and diagnostics: Feature views, transformation starts and results, purchase events, ad outcomes, errors, device/app information, and general usage events collected through Firebase Analytics.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: Used only when you choose to take a portrait inside MorphMe.
  • Photos and media: Used when you choose a portrait or save/share a generated result.
  • Internet and network access: Used to send transformation requests, receive results, load rewarded ads, process purchases, and record analytics.

3. How we use portraits

Portraits are transmitted to our backend and AI processing providers to create the transformation you request. We do not sell your portraits or use them for unrelated advertising. Submitted portraits and generated results may be retained temporarily as needed to process the request and make the result available.

4. Rewarded advertising

MorphMe may offer an optional rewarded advertisement to reveal a generated result. Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device/network signals, consent choices, and information about ad requests, impressions, clicks, and earned rewards. Ad personalization depends on your region, consent, and device settings.

5. In-app purchases

MorphMe offers optional premium purchases or subscriptions through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. MorphMe receives purchase and entitlement information needed to activate premium access, verify an active subscription, acknowledge purchases, and restore access.

6. Local storage

MorphMe may store transformation history, result references, app preferences, premium status, daily usage counters, and review-related state on your device. Local data is generally removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

7. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob: Rewarded advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and—where permitted—ad personalization. See Google’s advertising policies.
  • Google Play: In-app purchases, subscription management, and in-app review prompts.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: Product analytics, usage measurement, and diagnostics.
  • AI processing providers: Portrait transformation and result generation.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We disclose information only to service providers and processors as needed for portrait transformation, result delivery, rewarded advertising, purchases, analytics, security, and legal compliance.

9. Retention and deletion

Retention periods depend on the type of data and the service needed. Local app data remains until it is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Server-side portraits and generated results may be retained temporarily for processing and result delivery. To request deletion of data associated with your app-specific identifier, contact us below.

10. Children’s privacy

MorphMe is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Additional minimum-age rules may apply in your country.

11. Entertainment and AI notice

MorphMe is an entertainment application. Generated images are synthetic AI interpretations and may be inaccurate or unrealistic. They do not establish a person’s identity, gender identity, biological sex, or actual age.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted network transmission where supported. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as MorphMe changes. The revised version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
