Style Shift Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Style Shift ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Style Shift does not require account registration. The app uses the following information to provide and improve its image generation features:

  • Photos and generated images: Images you take or select as references for hairstyle, hair color, seasonal fashion, and occasion fashion generation, plus the AI-generated results created from those references.
  • App preferences and identifiers: Language, selected or detected country/region, onboarding progress, premium status, daily usage state, app version, and an app-specific device identifier.
  • Advertising data: Advertising identifiers and related signals may be accessed by Google AdMob to provide, measure, and improve ads, including personalized ads where permitted by law and your consent/settings.
  • Purchase data: Google Play subscription and purchase status used to provide premium features and verify whether your subscription is active.
  • Analytics data: App events such as feature views, scan starts, scan results, purchases, errors, and general usage events collected through Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Notifications: If enabled, the app may use notification status and local job information to send reminders when a generated result is ready or has not yet been opened.
  • Optional feedback: If you send feedback, we receive the message you write, your app version, and an app-specific identifier.

2. Permissions We Use

The app may request permissions only when they are needed for app features:

  • Camera: Used when you choose to take a reference photo inside the app.
  • Photos / storage: Used when you choose an image from your device or save a generated result to your gallery. On older Android versions, storage permission may be required to save images.
  • Notifications: Used to send result-ready reminders if you allow notifications.
  • Internet and network access: Used to upload images, receive generated results, show ads, process purchases, and use analytics.

3. How We Use Your Photos

Photos you submit are sent to our server solely to create AI-generated style transformations and related result previews. We do not use your photos for unrelated purposes, and we do not sell your photos.

4. Local Data Storage

Generation history, thumbnails, result references, app preferences, premium status, notification reminder state, daily usage counters, and review-related state may be stored locally on your device. This information is removed when you uninstall the application.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements, measure ad performance, prevent abuse, and personalize ad content where permitted.
  • Google Play: Used for in-app purchases, subscription verification, and review prompts.
  • Google Firebase Analytics: Used for app analytics and product improvement.
  • AI processing providers: Used to generate style transformation images from your submitted references.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal data. We only share information as needed to provide image generation, advertising, purchases, review prompts, analytics, notifications, downloads, and optional feedback handling.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Photos and generated results sent to our servers may be kept temporarily to process your request and make your result available in the app. If you want to request deletion of data associated with your app-specific identifier or submitted feedback, contact us using the email address below.

8. Children's Privacy

Style Shift is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Style Disclaimer

Generated images are AI-created visual previews for inspiration and entertainment. Results may vary based on lighting, pose, image quality, and selected options.

10. Security

Photos and generation requests are transmitted over a secure connection. If you send feedback, the message, app version, and app-specific identifier may be stored on our servers to review reports and reduce abuse. Photos submitted for processing are kept temporarily on our servers only for the duration of the generation process and subsequent history availability.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
